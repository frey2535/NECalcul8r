NECalcul8r is an electrical calculator and learning tool for electricians, electrical contractors, apprentices, inspectors, technicians, and electrical engineering students.

Perform NEC-based electrical calculations and learn how the results are determined with step-by-step math and electrical code references.

Built for the jobsite and the classroom:
• Electrical calculators for practical field and learning applications
• Step-by-step calculation explanations
• NEC tables and references where supported
• Tools to help understand electrical design and installation calculations
• Mobile-friendly access for electricians and apprentices

Whether you're learning electrical fundamentals, reviewing a calculation, or planning an installation, NECalcul8r helps you understand the math behind the answer.

Always verify results against the locally adopted National Electrical Code edition, applicable amendments, manufacturer instructions, and your authority having jurisdiction.
